# Changelog (for the owner, newest first)

Updated at every checkpoint. One short section per working day or session. Technical detail is in `docs/handoff.md` (Done) and `docs/skills.md`.

## 2026-11-20
- **Free Drive in Neon Harbor (first playable version).** A new main-menu button puts your garage car in the open city: about 6 km of streets in seven districts with over 10,000 buildings, the Bay Bridge and the harbour quay. The city streams in around you, buildings and the water's edge are solid, grass slows you down. R or the Respawn button puts you back on the nearest road. Pause (Esc) has **fast travel** to the six districts, a **photo** button (saves a picture without the menus), Respawn and Menu. Each district now has its own look: glass towers downtown, pink neon street lights on the Strip, brick in the Old Town, metal sheds at the harbour; the fog changes colour as you cross districts, streets turn glossy in the rain and white in the snow, and street lights line every road. Neon shop signs (made-up brands) light up the Strip, the old town and downtown. **Traffic** now drives the streets: cars keep their lane, stop at red lights and for you, give way at junctions and slow down for bends (they never corner harder than your car could); hit one and it stops for a moment. **People** walk the sidewalks and step back when a car comes close (nobody gets run over). A **minimap** turns with your car, and Pause > **Map** shows the whole city: tap a place and the GPS draws the way (it finds a new way if you leave the route, and says when you have arrived). **Speed cameras** on the fastest straights show your speed as you pass, and your best for each camera. Still to come: drift zones, a car meet, missions and saving your city records.
- **Own online lobby (in progress, not on the server yet).** A small lobby server of our own: public "Open rooms" list and a **Quick race** button (joins the newest open room or opens one), while room codes with friends keep working. **If the host leaves mid-race, the race goes on**: another player takes over the room (only on our own server). If our server is not reachable the game uses the old connection method automatically. Needs your OK to install (`scripts/setup-server-lobby.sh`) and the administrator's `/ws/` line; please read `docs/online-protocol.md`.
- **The website is up to date again** (2026-11-20): Chapter 3, cinematics, the feedback form, the new music and the health page are live. A quick check page (`/api/health.php`) shows the database, disk space and the age of the last backup.
- **Backups are running.** The server now saves the game database every night (14 days kept) and the first backup was restored into a test copy that matched exactly. Our database log is rotated weekly.
- **Online lap boards (ready, one OK needed).** Your best lap per map, weather and car class can go to an online board: the map screen shows the top 3, the results say "#3 of 41", and the daily challenge has its own board. Fake times (too fast, teleports, wrong way round) are refused; the boards say "unverified times" because nobody checks by hand. Laps driven offline are sent later. To switch it on, OK the command `scripts/apply-server-schema.sh` (it makes a backup first, then adds three tables).
- **Better start prompt (version 2)** in `docs/prompts/everything.md`: has an APPROVALS line (say up front what server changes are OK), keeps working when an item is waiting for someone, reads less, and ends every session with the same short report.
- **Server housekeeping prepared (not installed yet, needs your OK).** Nightly database backup (kept 14 days) with an automatic restore test, weekly log rotation for our MySQL log, and a health page (`/api/health.php`: database, disk, backup age, version) with a checker `scripts/health-check.sh`. A list of every file we have on the server is in `server/README.md`. The text for the server administrator (close old database ports, cache headers, WebSocket lines) is ready in `docs/admin-request.md`.
- **Found while checking the server (read-only):** the game already sees each player's real address (the "everyone looks the same" effect was just testers on the office network), and port 8000 belongs to the administrator's metrics service, so our future lobby will use port 8770.
- **Songs change by themselves.** You never need "Next song" again. The music shuffles (every song once before any repeats, never the same song twice in a row) and blends into the next one: the old song drops its drums and goes muffled while the new one fades in, so there is no hard cut. New default style **Auto** mixes the deep-house songs with the synthwave track (Settings > Advanced > Sound > Music style: Auto / Deep house / Synthwave). "Next song" is still there as an extra. If you had Deep house before, you keep Deep house, which now also changes by itself.
- **Four new songs** (9 deep-house songs now): Glass Tower, Afterglow, Velvet Underpass, Last Exit, from 112 to 128 BPM. The synthwave track (now called "Neon Grid") was about 10 dB quieter than the house songs; it now plays at the same loudness.
- **"Now playing" line.** A small line at the top shows the song name for 4 seconds when a song changes. Turn it off in Settings > Advanced > Sound.
- Fixed: after a stutter (e.g. while a track loads) the music no longer plays a burst of late notes at once.
- To listen to the song transitions without playing: `logs/music/join-*.wav` (made by `tests/lab/music-join.mjs`).
- **Master plan and one prompt for everything.** `docs/master-plan.md` lists the five big jobs (songs that change by themselves, server settings, leaderboards, own online lobby, open-world city driving) with the status of each step; `docs/prompts/everything.md` is the prompt to paste into a new session and to resume later.
- **Faster launches.** The game now downloads only what changed since last time (about 28 bytes when nothing did, instead of ~526 KB for a big save), and big saves are compressed both ways. The server part is already live; the browser part ships with the next deploy.
- **Documentation system.** This changelog, the owner's guide, the online/Redis roadmap and a ready-made prompt for the next big job. Rule: docs are updated after every change, and every session ends with a checkpoint.
- **Decisions:** no Redis yet; own lobby server later (see `docs/roadmap-online.md`).
- Git now works from the WSL terminal; everything so far is committed and pushed.

## 2026-11-19 (big day)

- **Modes and feel:** manual gearbox (E/Q, d-pad, phone + and -) with "perfect shift" nitro rewards; two curves-only drift maps (Drift Ring, Serpent Drift); quick tutorial with a language step; subtitles off by default.

- **Settings and menus:** Basic / Advanced tabs, Auto graphics and frame cap (starts at Medium on real GPUs, adjusts both ways), Unlock all cars back, cleaner garage and tuning screens.
- **Sound:** recorded engines pitched less shrill (per-car trim and filter, Evo darkest). Not heard by anyone yet.
- **Site:** deployed to https://notcarlos.mosidealus.nl (firewall ports 80/443 opened; before that it only worked from the company network).
