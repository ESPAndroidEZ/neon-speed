# Changelog (for the owner, newest first)

Updated at every checkpoint. One short section per working day or session. Technical detail is in `docs/handoff.md` (Done) and `docs/skills.md`.

## 2026-11-23
- **Phone screens fixed (first batch).** On a phone held sideways the garage is now a narrow panel on the left with the car free on the right and the buttons pinned at the bottom; the pause menu fits in two columns; small buttons got bigger tap areas; the "Now playing" line no longer covers titles. The Free Drive street card, race offer and pause were also reworked. A full list of what is still open is in `docs/ui-story.md`.
- **Story plan for chapters 4 and 5** written in `docs/story.md` (new characters Pip, Wren and Madame Adeyemi); not in the game yet.
- **Free Drive jobs.** Driving into downtown, the harbour, old town or the Strip can offer a timed job (press G or tap Go): get a parcel to the meet, reach the drift pad, a speed camera or a token before the clock runs out. The map shows the route; success pays $1,200-2,000 once. Jobs are plain data (`data/world/missions.ts`), more can be added without code.
- **Free Drive screen restyled** in the menu look: round icon buttons (respawn, pause) with the token count on top, cleaner street line and race offer card.
- **Neon tokens in Free Drive (a point to the city).** 50 glowing diamonds float over streets in downtown, the strip, old town and the harbour. Drive through them: a pop-up counts them, 10 / 25 / 50 tokens pay $1,000 / $2,500 / $6,000 once. Collected tokens are saved.
- **Hitting traffic now does something.** A car you hit is shoved along the crash, spins, rests for a moment and then drives back into its lane (no snapping). Sparks, shake and sound were already there. Free Drive only; race handling is unchanged.

## 2026-11-21
- **Live on the website.** Free Drive in Neon Harbor, city races, the new details and all fixes are now on notcarlos.mosidealus.nl. The online lap boards are switched on (empty until people race). Our own online lobby is running on the server; players reach it as soon as the administrator adds one web-server line (until then online races use the old peer-to-peer method, as before).
- **Neon Harbor looks lived-in.** Roofs have air-conditioning units, water tanks and antennas; sidewalks have benches, bins, fire hydrants and bus shelters; eight new shop signs (fish market, sailor bar, 24 h diner, tyre shop, bakery, tattoo, karaoke...), and long shop names no longer run off their signs. Traffic is no longer one car copied 40 times: hatchbacks, vans, yellow taxis and pickups, each as solid as it looks.
- **Snow looks right on street lights:** posts and benches no longer turn into glowing white bars in snow (only the tops get snow).
- **You know where you are.** Free Drive shows the street and district above the minimap ("Pier Road · Harbour") and a short line about a district when you drive into it, in English and Spanish.

## 2026-11-20
- **Free Drive checked in every district and weather.** The hills had ground covering parts of the road and odd pale wedges at crossings: fixed. The "Now playing" song line no longer covers the Pause button in Free Drive. Draw calls stay well inside the budget everywhere (about 150 of 250 on Low).
- **City races in Free Drive.** Six gold rings in Neon Harbor, one per district. Stop in one and you can start a street race (2 laps, 3 rivals in cars like yours) on a loop the GPS found through that district's streets. It pays like a free race, remembers your best time, and afterwards "Back to Free Drive" puts you where you started. Not on the website yet.
- **Free Drive in Neon Harbor (first playable version).** A new main-menu button puts your garage car in the open city: about 6 km of streets in seven districts with over 10,000 buildings, the Bay Bridge and the harbour quay. The city streams in around you, buildings and the water's edge are solid, grass slows you down. R or the Respawn button puts you back on the nearest road. Pause (Esc) has **fast travel** to the six districts, a **photo** button (saves a picture without the menus), Respawn and Menu. Each district now has its own look: glass towers downtown, pink neon street lights on the Strip, brick in the Old Town, metal sheds at the harbour; the fog changes colour as you cross districts, streets turn glossy in the rain and white in the snow, and street lights line every road. Neon shop signs (made-up brands) light up the Strip, the old town and downtown. **Traffic** now drives the streets: cars keep their lane, stop at red lights and for you, give way at junctions and slow down for bends (they never corner harder than your car could); hit one and it stops for a moment. **People** walk the sidewalks and step back when a car comes close (nobody gets run over). A **minimap** turns with your car, and Pause > **Map** shows the whole city: tap a place and the GPS draws the way (it finds a new way if you leave the route, and says when you have arrived). **Speed cameras** on the fastest straights show your speed as you pass, and your best for each camera. Six glowing **drift zones** in the industrial area and the harbour score the drift points you earn inside them, and the harbour has a **car meet**: park among eight show cars for a moment and the crew notices your ride. Your **city records are saved** now (camera speeds, drift-zone scores, the car meet), and Free Drive starts where you left it last time. Still to come: missions and city races (jumps need a decision first).
- **Own online lobby (in progress, not on the server yet).** A small lobby server of our own: public "Open rooms" list and a **Quick race** button (joins the newest open room or opens one), while room codes with friends keep working. **If the host leaves mid-race, the race goes on**: another player takes over the room (only on our own server). If our server is not reachable the game uses the old connection method automatically. Needs your OK to install (`scripts/setup-server-lobby.sh`) and the administrator's `/ws/` line; please read `docs/online-protocol.md`.
- **The website is up to date again** (2026-11-20): Chapter 3, cinematics, the feedback form, the new music and the health page are live. A quick check page (`/api/health.php`) shows the database, disk space and the age of the last backup.
- **Backups are running.** The server now saves the game database every night (14 days kept) and the first backup was restored into a test copy that matched exactly. Our database log is rotated weekly.
- **Online lap boards (ready, one OK needed).** Your best lap per map, weather and car class can go to an online board: the map screen shows the top 3, the results say "#3 of 41", and the daily challenge has its own board. Fake times (too fast, teleports, wrong way round) are refused; the boards say "unverified times" because nobody checks by hand. Laps driven offline are sent later. To switch it on, OK the command `scripts/apply-server-schema.sh` (it makes a backup first, then adds three tables).
- **Better start prompt (version 2)** in `docs/prompts/everything.md`: has an APPROVALS line (say up front what server changes are OK), keeps working when an item is waiting for someone, reads less, and ends every session with the same short report.
- **Server housekeeping prepared (not installed yet, needs your OK).** Nightly database backup (kept 14 days) with an automatic restore test, weekly log rotation for our MySQL log, and a health page (`/api/health.php`: database, disk, backup age, version) with a checker `scripts/health-check.sh`. A list of every file we have on the server is in `server/README.md`. The text for the server administrator (close old database ports, cache headers, WebSocket lines) is ready in `docs/admin-request.md`.
- **Found while checking the server (read-only):** the game already sees each player's real address (the "everyone looks the same" effect was just testers on the office network), and port 8000 belongs to the administrator's metrics service, so our future lobby will use port 8770.
- **Songs change by themselves.** You never need "Next song" again. The music shuffles (every song once before any repeats, never the same song twice in a row) and blends into the next one: the old song drops its drums and goes muffled while the new one fades in, so there is no hard cut. New default style **Auto** mixes the deep-house songs with the synthwave track (Settings > Advanced > Sound > Music style: Auto / Deep house / Synthwave). "Next song" is still there as an extra. If you had Deep house before, you keep Deep house, which now also changes by itself.
- **Four new songs** (9 deep-house songs now): Glass Tower, Afterglow, Velvet Underpass, Last Exit, from 112 to 128 BPM. The synthwave track (now called "Neon Grid") was about 10 dB quieter than the house songs; it now plays at the same loudness.
- **"Now playing" line.** A small line at the top shows the song name for 4 seconds when a song changes. Turn it off in Settings > Advanced > Sound.
- Fixed: after a stutter (e.g. while a track loads) the music no longer plays a burst of late notes at once.
- To listen to the song transitions without playing: `logs/music/join-*.wav` (made by `tests/lab/music-join.mjs`).
- **Master plan and one prompt for everything.** `docs/master-plan.md` lists the five big jobs (songs that change by themselves, server settings, leaderboards, own online lobby, open-world city driving) with the status of each step; `docs/prompts/everything.md` is the prompt to paste into a new session and to resume later.
- **Faster launches.** The game now downloads only what changed since last time (about 28 bytes when nothing did, instead of ~526 KB for a big save), and big saves are compressed both ways. The server part is already live; the browser part ships with the next deploy.
- **Documentation system.** This changelog, the owner's guide, the online/Redis roadmap and a ready-made prompt for the next big job. Rule: docs are updated after every change, and every session ends with a checkpoint.
- **Decisions:** no Redis yet; own lobby server later (see `docs/roadmap-online.md`).
- Git now works from the WSL terminal; everything so far is committed and pushed.
