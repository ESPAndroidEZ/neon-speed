# Engine sounds: what is weak (all 8 cars have recorded loops since 2026-11-16)

Sources and licences: docs/audio-credits.md. Re-cutting: scripts/engine-loops/README.md. Nobody has listened yet.

- supra: no free 2JZ-GTE recording was usable (the A80 clip is mostly wind noise); uses a Lexus IS300 (2JZ-GE, NA).
- civic: idle borrowed from a Lancer idle (the Civic idle clips found were ambiguous); revs are a K20 (FN2), not a B16.
- evo: nothing between idle (732) and 4119 rpm: the 1500-3500 range is pitched from those two loops.
- e61: only 3 loops (834, 1507, 3685 rpm), all 'both'/'on': no off-throttle loop, nothing near the 5000 redline.
- rx7, supra: no off-throttle loops (lifting uses the on-load loops, quieter).
- golf: the Mk6 GTI is a 2.0 TSI with an aftermarket exhaust (louder than a stock Golf IV 1.8T).
- Loops are 0.5-1.5 s; if a car sounds "buzzy" or repetitive, cut longer windows from steadier parts.
Better sources: Freesound "<car> onboard steady rpm", Soundholder packs (CC BY 3.0), Commons "engine and exhaust sound".
