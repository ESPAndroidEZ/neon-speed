## Write
1. `qwen-save.mts` (src/core/save.ts, src/core/gameSave.ts): SaveStore defaults for every settings field; `parseSave` with each field of the wrong type (string where number, null, array, nested garbage) falls back field by field; owned list de-duplicated and filtered to known cars; `selected` falls back to an owned car; paint and glow clamped to 0..0xffffff; best lap below 5 s rejected; `recordRace` with NaN or negative cash does not corrupt cash; `SaveSlots`: `put` to each slot, `latest()` picks the newest including ties, putting a newer autosave does not touch manual slots, `parseSnapshot` rejects a snapshot missing each required field one at a time (loop over field names), accepts extra unknown fields.
2. `qwen-race.mts` (src/core/raceManager.ts, raceResult.ts, lapCounter.ts): `LapCounter` with a track of 100 samples: start behind the line, going backwards below the start never gives negative laps, `checkpoint` stays in 0..checkpoints-1 over 3 laps, a teleport does not add progress; `positionOf` ordering with finishers (by finish rank) ahead of unfinished (by progress); countdown lasts exactly 3 s of `step` calls (180 steps of 1/60) and cars do not move until it ends; `banner` strings ('3','2','1','GO!','' after 0.8 s); `buildResult` cash for positions 1 to 6 is non-increasing and never negative; a race with 1 lap finishes the player when the lap counter reaches 1.
3. `qwen-track.mts` (src/world/streetPath.ts, track.ts, trackQuery.ts, kestrelBay.ts): `buildStreetPath` on a 100 x 100 m square with r = 10 corners gives min radius about 10 and a closed loop (last point close to the first); a vertex list with too large radius is clamped (`minRadius` smaller than requested); `createTrackFromPath` spacing is uniform within 1%; `curvatureAt` sign: a counter-clockwise (left-turning) square has positive curvature on its arcs; `locate` with a full search and with a hint return the same sample for 50 random points near the track; lateral offset sign for points left/right of the centre line; `halfWidthAt` equals width/2; every `track.wall[i]` is greater than `halfWidthAt`; zone list covers every sample; the Kestrel Bay start line (sample 0) has its tangent pointing along +X.
