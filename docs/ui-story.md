# UI and story review

## 1. UI audit

Captured 2026-11-23 with Playwright (touch emulation under 1100 px) from the dev server, no source edits. 11 screens x 2 languages x 8 viewports (360x640, 390x844, 740x360, 844x390, 932x430, 1024x768, 1366x768, 1920x1080) = 190+ PNGs in `logs/qa/ui-audit/<lang>-<WxH>-<screen>.png` (max 1280 px wide) plus `audit-*.json` (scroll, tap target, font and clip metrics per screen). Screens: main, garage (also the car select), maps (track select), weather (after picking a map), tune, settings-basic, settings-adv, story, howto (opened from Crew, step 2), pause, results.

Caveats: the dev flags (`?screen=`, `?results`, `?pause=menu`) skip the real flow, so some leaks (title "RACE / ENTER" bar behind menus, touch pads behind results, crew screen behind the tutorial) may be dev-only; confirm in the real flow (marked "verify"). Portrait and desktop runs were captured while three browsers ran in parallel, so some of their JSON metrics were taken with the loading overlay up; the phone-landscape runs (740x360, 844x390, 932x430) were redone sequentially and are reliable. Metrics use 44 px for tap targets and 11.5 px as the small-text limit.

### Screens with no major problems

Main (portrait and desktop), pause on 360x640 and above, settings tabs at 1024x768 and above, story at 932x430 and above (cards fit).

### Suggested order of work

1. Garage: move the prev/next/select/back actions into a fixed footer outside the scrolling panel and make the panel narrow in landscape (rows 1, 2, 10, 18).
2. Top bar: remove the ghost RACE/ENTER button from menus, shrink the bar to 44 px, move the music toast to the bottom or the HUD corner (rows 3, 4).
3. Pause: two-column list in landscape (row 5).
4. Tuning and settings: sticky compact header, row tap areas of 44 px, 12 px minimum text (rows 6, 7, 8, 9).
5. Maps: horizontal snap carousel; fix the 63 / 45 % snow grip mismatch (rows 11, 12).
6. Verify rows 3, 13 and 14 in the real flow (without dev flags) before editing.

## 2. Decisions and status (2026-11-23)
- Rows 3, 10, 13, 14 were dev-flag artefacts or already dev-only (`import.meta.env.DEV` guards the glb/procedural hints); not changed.
- Done (CSS first, no screen rewrites): `ui/phoneCss.ts` (injected with the menu CSS): garage panel narrow on the left on short landscape screens with the action row pinned (`.rb-gact`), two-column pause (`.rb-pause`, race pause and Free Drive pause), 12 px minimum help text, 44 px hit areas for swatches (pseudo-element) and language buttons; "Now playing" moved to the bottom centre everywhere (rows 1, 2, 4, 5, 8 partly, 9 partly, 17). Free Drive: street card top-centre, compact offer card, no Enter hint on touch (`ui/roamCss.ts`).
- NOT done yet: tuning sticky compact header and 44 px upgrade rows (6, 8), settings tabs by topic and text-size option (7), map list as a snap carousel and the snow 63/45 % stat mismatch (11, 12), story card layout and contrast (15), main-menu hierarchy and desktop scaling (16, 19), portrait garage (18).
- The design system tokens already exist in `ui/theme.ts` (spacing, type, colours, radii, motion); new component files (Toast, bottom sheet, tab bar) are NOT built: existing `uiKit` buttons, panels, toggles and sliders in `menuCss.ts` are reused.
- Story (section 6 of the brief): arc designed in `docs/story.md` ("Season 2 arc"); chapters 4 and 5, codex screen, recap card and woven tutorial are NOT built.
