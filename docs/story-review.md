# Story review (before the 2026-11-16 rewrite)

Honest critique of the Chapter 1 + 2 story as it stood (`src/data/story*.ts`, `lore.ts`, `radio*.ts`, `barks.ts`).

## What works
- The setup is clean and readable: a port city, a debt, a borrowed car, one big villain. Players get it in 30 seconds.
- Switch as a pirate-radio narrator is a strong device, and "Switch is Cain" is a real, emotional reveal.
- Chapter 2's choice (`open` / `sponsor`) is a good idea and the flags and relationships are saved properly.
- The rivals have clear driving identities (Jinx rain, Tank blocker, Vex drift, Brick never lifts).

## What is weak or generic
- **Slow start.** A first launch plays five lore cards, a narrator line and three phone lines before the player touches a pedal. Controls are explained in one wall of text ("brake early, throw it in, use the handbrake...").
- **Dante has no inner life.** He is "calm, polite, ruthless" and says villain lines ("a street is only worth what it pays"). The motive "he wants to own everything" is not a reason to care.
- **The mystery has no clues.** Chapter 2 ends with "the crash was never an accident" and a promise of a Chapter 3 that does not exist. The player never gets a clue, a suspect or a payoff.
- **Rivals are interchangeable props.** Jinx, Tank and Vex say almost nothing outside bark lines. Nova, Brick and Calloway each get one scene and are then gone. Nobody changes.
- **The branch is cosmetic.** `open` and `sponsor` only swap three epilogue lines. Same rivals, same race, same card tone.
- **Repeat attempts are dead.** A lost event replays the SAME line every time ("Again."). Banter ignores weather, car and earlier results.
- **No ending.** The game ends on a "Chapter 3 is coming" card.

## Where players would skip
- The "Previously in Kestrel Bay" cards before the first race (five cards of backstory before any play).
- Mara's long phone lines (25-35 words) and the Dante speech before the finale.

## Plot holes and inconsistencies
- Mara says Cain "vanished", yet Switch knows Cain's sayings and Mara never notices the voice. Nothing plants it.
- Dante hands over "a key to the East Yard" in the Chapter 1 finale. The key is never used again.
- The Rookie "drives like Cain" with no reason given.
- Mara forgives the twelve years of silence in no scene (she says nothing to Cain at all).
- Calloway's heir "paid for the guard rail" but has no stake in the mystery; the Chapter 3 outline makes his father the culprit, but nothing in Chapter 2 points there.
- Lore (`story.md`) still said "Racer's Blood" is the Red Hand Wall. The game is Neon Speed now.

## Verdict
Good bones, no spine. The rewrite keeps every event id, the flags, the save format and the rivals, and adds: a cold open, a hidden-in-plain-sight mystery with clues in every district, a human antagonist, a betrayal (Cain's silence) that Mara has to work through, a branch that changes the final race and the ending, and lines that react to the car, results and retries.
